# Field Notebook — A5 Hardcover

A durable A5 hardcover notebook built for daily writing, sketching, and field work. 192 numbered pages of acid-free 100 gsm paper, lay-flat binding, and a built-in ribbon marker.

## Specifications

- **Format:** A5 (148 × 210 mm)
- **Pages:** 192 numbered, dot-grid (5 mm spacing)
- **Paper:** 100 gsm, acid-free, fountain-pen friendly
- **Cover:** Hardcover, linen-bound, water-resistant
- **Binding:** Smyth-sewn, lays completely flat
- **Extras:** Ribbon marker, elastic closure, back pocket
- **Weight:** 380 g

## What's in the Box

- 1× Field Notebook (A5 Hardcover)
- 1× Reference card (dot-grid layouts and proofing marks)
- 1× Sticker sheet

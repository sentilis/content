## About

**Voltaire Énergie** is a Paris-based industrial climate-tech startup helping European manufacturers cut energy consumption by 18–35% through real-time IoT monitoring and AI-driven optimization. Member of *La French Tech 2030* and *Mission French Tech Next40/120 (Tremplin)*. Backed by BPI France, Eurazeo, and Kima Ventures.

## Mission

Decarbonize European heavy industry by turning every factory floor into a smart, self-optimizing energy system — without ripping and replacing existing equipment.

## Product

### Voltaire OS
- **Connects** to legacy PLCs and modern sensors via OPC-UA, Modbus, and MQTT.
- **Forecasts** energy demand at 15-minute granularity with proprietary ML models.
- **Optimizes** HVAC, compressed-air, and process loads in real time.
- **Reports** carbon footprint aligned with CSRD and GHG Protocol standards.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, RGPD-compliant, hosted on sovereign EU cloud (OVHcloud).

## Traction

- **€6.2M ARR** as of Q1 2026, with 96% gross retention.
- **42 industrial sites** deployed across France, Germany, and the Benelux.
- **128 GWh** of energy saved cumulatively — equivalent to ~22,000 tons of CO₂e avoided.
- **Reference clients:** Saint-Gobain, ArcelorMittal (pilot), Michelin (pilot).

## Team

A multidisciplinary team of 28 based in Paris (Station F) and Lyon. Engineers, data scientists, and energy specialists from Schneider Electric, EDF, Polytechnique, Mines ParisTech, and INRIA.

## Funding

- **Seed** — €3.5M led by Elaia Partners (February 2024).
- **Subventions:** €1.2M from ADEME (Agence de la transition écologique) and the *France 2030* plan.

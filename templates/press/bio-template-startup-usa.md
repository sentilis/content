## About

**Northwind Labs** is a San Francisco–based startup building the AI-native customer support platform for high-growth B2B SaaS companies. We help support teams resolve tickets 5x faster while keeping a human in the loop for sensitive cases. Backed by Y Combinator (W24) and tier-1 US investors.

## Mission

Make world-class customer support an unfair advantage for every software company — regardless of headcount.

## Product

### Northwind Resolve
- **Deploys** in under 30 minutes via native integrations with Zendesk, Intercom, and HubSpot.
- **Automates** Tier-1 ticket resolution with company-grounded LLM agents.
- **Escalates** complex or sensitive cases to humans with full conversation context.
- **Provides** SOC 2 Type II compliance, EU/US data residency, and BYO-LLM support.

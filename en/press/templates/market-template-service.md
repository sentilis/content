```markdown
# Backend Architecture Audit

A focused, two-week engagement in which I review your backend codebase, infrastructure, and on-call practices, then deliver a prioritized action plan you can execute with your existing team.

## Who It's For

- Series A / B startups whose backend was built fast and now feels fragile.
- Engineering leaders preparing for a 10x traffic increase or a major migration.
- Teams that want a senior outside perspective before committing to a rewrite.

## What's Included

- **Kickoff call (90 min):** Goals, constraints, and access setup.
- **Codebase review:** Up to 80k LOC across services, data layer, and infra-as-code.
- **Production walkthrough:** Live tour of dashboards, alerts, and the last 3 incidents.
- **5 one-hour interviews** with engineers and stakeholders.
- **Written report (25–40 pages):** Findings, risks ranked by impact, and a 90-day roadmap.
- **Closing workshop (2 h):** Walkthrough with your team, Q&A, and prioritization.

## Deliverables

- Architecture diagram (current state and target state).
- Top 10 risks with severity, effort, and recommended owner.
- Quick wins shippable in under one sprint.
- 30/60/90-day implementation roadmap.

## Timeline & Process

- **Week 1:** Discovery, access, interviews, codebase reading.
- **Week 2:** Drafting, validation calls, final report and workshop.

## Pricing

Flat fee of **€2,400** for the full engagement. Includes one follow-up call 30 days after delivery.

## How to Book

Reply to this listing or write to `hello@example.com` with a short description of your stack and the outcome you're after. I take on a maximum of two audits per month.
```

> 💡 **Tip:** Service listings don't require an `attachment`. If you want a downloadable scope-of-work PDF, switch `kind` to `digital` and add `attachment: ./attachments/attachment.pdf`.

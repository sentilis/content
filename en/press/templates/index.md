# Sentilis Templates 🧩

A curated collection of ready-to-copy templates to help you publish faster on Sentilis. Each template is a self-contained article with a copyable Markdown block — open it, copy the block, paste it into a new file, and tweak the placeholders.

## 💼 Bio Templates

Profiles for people and companies. Pair them with the [Bio docs](https://sentilis.me/en/press/what-is-a-bio-6a016eb9550ca18de606688f) to learn about frontmatter and the directory layout.

- [Bio Template: Software Developer](./bio-template-software-developer.md) — Senior engineer profile with experience, education, certifications, and open-source work.
- [Bio Template: US Startup](./bio-template-startup-usa.md) — Company profile / investor one-pager for a US-based startup.

## 🛒 Market Templates

One template per `kind`. See the [Market docs](https://sentilis.me/en/press/what-is-market-6a016eba550ca18de6066893) for validation rules and the `attachments/` layout.

- [Market Template: Service](./market-template-service.md) — Consulting or freelance engagement (`kind: service`).
- [Market Template: Physical Product](./market-template-product.md) — Storable, shippable goods (`kind: product`).
- [Market Template: Digital Product](./market-template-digital.md) — Downloadable file with a mandatory `attachment` (`kind: digital`).

## 📰 Press Templates

Three opening structures for the most common Press formats. See the [Press docs](https://sentilis.me/en/press/what-is-a-press-69f1aa4c8d8ef9e4cd7491c8) for frontmatter options and directory rules.

- [Press Template: Press Release](./press-template-press-release.md) — Official announcement with dateline, quotes, and boilerplate.
- [Press Template: Blog Post / Long-Form Article](./press-template-blog-post.md) — Technical deep dive or opinion piece with hook → problem → approach → takeaway.
- [Press Template: Changelog / Quick Note](./press-template-changelog.md) — Short, scannable update with New / Improved / Fixed / Deprecated sections.
